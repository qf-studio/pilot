## Summary
Cap-stalled task re-arm (operator recipe, restart-proof). When tasks hit the repick cap and flip stalled (escalate-and-hold) after an outage, a plain daemon restart does NOT re-admit them. Three required steps: (1) remove any pilot-blocked labels; (2) flip cap-stalled execution rows stalled->failed by EXACT execution ID with an audit note; (3) zero the repick_backoff counters. Next dispatcher tick then grants fresh generations and re-picks. Verified during 2026-08-23 OAuth outage recovery; pairs with the OAuth-expiry pitfall (mem-171).
