# Learning: After a merge of a base change, hold the leg that builds on it until the run-it 

## Summary
After a merge of a base change, hold the leg that builds on it until the run-it review of the base PR reports, and put the review findings into the text of that leg before the dispatch. On 2026-11-19 and 11-20 this caught a real gap in each round before any run used the code: the read-only token needed three rounds (repository not bound to the config, owner not checked in the answer, then safe with conditions), the gate command seam needed two (reads of the task directory outside the seam, then the proof tests). The hold costs about 25 minutes and the queue is rarely empty.

## Context
Discovered during development.
