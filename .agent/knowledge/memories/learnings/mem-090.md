## Recommended Approach
A .zip 'fix' from a non-collaborator on an issue you just filed is a payload, not a contribution — never download/apply it; implement from the spec you authored. Full procedure in SOP sops/security/malicious-patch-zip-comments.md. Re-enabling is safe (restart daemon + re-label); comments can't reach execution.
