# Learning: Status reports to Aleks must be scannable state lists, not narrative prose

## Summary
When reporting pipeline/queue/delivery state, Aleks needs a scannable list grouped by required action, not analytic prose. Direct feedback (2026-07-13, verbatim): "This gives me 0 info. Could you remember to make me scanable lists on check, this is done, this is failed and needs to reset/queue again... I read the text and can't understand what is shipped and what is needed my attention."

## Context
After the overnight hardening run (6 issues shipped), a dense "watch item" paragraph mixed shipped PRs, superseded duplicates, ledger anomalies, and future caveats into one narrative block. The information was accurate but unusable for a quick scan.

## Recommended Approach
Structure every status/check report as grouped lists in this order:
1. ✅ **Done** — shipped/merged, zero action needed (issue → PR pairs, one line each)
2. ❌ **Failed / needs re-queue** — what failed, why, and the exact reset action
3. ⚠️ **Needs your attention** — decisions or manual actions only the user can take, imperative phrasing
4. 👀 **Watch** — no action now, what to look for and when (keep to 1-2 lines, or omit)
Analysis/forensics go AFTER the lists or on request, never instead of them. One item = one line. Never bury an action item inside a paragraph.
