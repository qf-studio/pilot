## Update 2026-11-16 evening (founder call, replaces the "wait for merge" part)

The founder does not want a question for each PR and does not want review verdict terms in reports ("no idea on Go or Go with notes, make it done as we have a plan"). Standing instruction for the agreed plan of TASK-651: when the review finds no blocker and CI is green, mark the PR ready (the daemon merges it), then file the next ticket of the plan. Ask the founder only when the review finds a blocker, when work leaves the plan, or for a step on the box. Report in plain words: what merged, what it does, what is next. The draft hold stays in place until the review is done.

## Update 2026-11-17 (founder call, replaces the draft hold)

What happened: three PRs of TASK-651 (6425, 6426, 6427) stayed in draft for about nine hours over the night of 11-16. The planning session waited for the founder's answer to a question about another PR (6424) and started no review for the three during that wait. The founder saw them in the board stage `merging` and wrote: "Why the hell you delay em? WE have opus on reviews, no?" and "This blocks the progress". The session released the three at once and the daemon merged them in two minutes.

Founder's point, in the founder's words: Pilot already reviews with Opus before the PR opens; a second hold blocks delivery.

Interpretation of the planning session (not the founder's words): no draft hold for the PRs of this plan. Pilot's review and green CI are sufficient for the merge. The run-it review runs after the merge, and each finding becomes a follow-up leg. Evidence for this: every finding of every run-it review of 11-16 and 11-17 became a follow-up leg, and the one blocker (PR 6424) got the founder answer "merge, then fix". The option `run_as_user` is refused by the preflight, so a merged defect cannot be used.

Proposed by the session and NOT confirmed by the founder: keep a hold for the one PR that removes the preflight refusal. Ask before that PR.

Also: never let an open question for one PR stop work on other PRs.

## Recommended Approach
No draft watcher. Dispatch; the daemon merges on green CI; run-it review after the merge; findings into follow-up legs; report in plain words.
