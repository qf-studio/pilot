## Problem

Part 2 of the platform-outage breaker (part 1 = #4791, which lands the correlation tracker and destructive-action suppression). This leg stops the daemon from *spending* during an outage and makes recovery automatic rather than operator-driven.

**Depends on #4791 being merged** — it extends the tracker/breaker that leg introduces.

## Scope — part 2 only

1. **External status probe (advisory, never required)**: when the breaker is about to open, and every M minutes while open (default 5), GET `https://www.githubstatus.com/api/v2/status.json` with a 5s timeout and explicit User-Agent (convention: `internal/health/health.go:141-166`). A `major`/`critical` indicator raises confidence and is included in the open alert; a green indicator does **not** veto the correlation signal (status pages lag — verified during the 08-06 incident). Probe failure is ignored entirely and never blocks opening.
2. **Pause new dispatch while open** via the existing chokepoint `Dispatcher.PauseAdmission()`/`ResumeAdmission()` (`internal/executor/dispatcher.go:2279-2306`, enforced at `ProjectWorker.processQueue:2557`). This is what stops burning executor spend on work that cannot pass CI. Config-gated, default on.
   - **Shared-owner safety**: the self-upgrade drain (`cmd/pilot/main.go:3428`, GH-4683) uses the same flag. Do not let two owners fight over one bool — reference-count or track owners explicitly, and test the interleaving.
   - **Verify reachability in polling mode** (the box's mode) before relying on it: the known call sites sit in dashboard-mode code.
3. **Suppress merges of already-green PRs while open** — during a platform incident the CI signal itself is untrustworthy.
4. **Held-PR re-drive on close**: PRs parked by the breaker re-enter `StageWaitingCI` when it closes, following the `reAdoptHeldRebasePR` revival shape (`controller.go:5009`) with a distinct hold flag and a capped re-adoption count.
5. Tests: probe failure does not block opening · green probe does not veto correlation · admission paused/resumed with the self-upgrade drain interleaved · held PRs re-driven exactly once on close · merge suppression while open · disabled-by-config is a byte-identical no-op · `-race` clean.
